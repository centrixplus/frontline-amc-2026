Preamble

The deployed solution has been operational at the Customer's premises since 23 April 2025, with the Vendor providing technical support throughout that period at no additional charge. This Agreement formalises the ongoing maintenance and support arrangement on a structured contractual basis with effect from the Effective Date stated above.

1. Scope of Solution Covered

1.1 Call Centre Solution

A proprietary call-centre / contact-centre platform owned and developed by the Vendor. The Customer is granted a license to use this software at the agreed location and user scale. The underlying intellectual property, source code, and all derivative rights to the Call Centre Solution remain the exclusive property of Centrix Plus.

1.2 CRM & Mobile Application

A CRM system and accompanying mobile application, designed and developed exclusively for Frontline Express. The Vendor undertakes that this custom-built CRM and Mobile Application shall not be licensed, sold, sub-licensed, or otherwise transferred to any third party during or after the term of this Agreement. The Vendor retains the underlying source code and technology rights; the Customer holds an exclusive, perpetual right of use for the deployed instance.

2. AMC Coverage — Included

  1. Software maintenance of the current production build of the Call Centre, CRM, and Mobile Application as deployed on the Effective Date.
  2. Bug fixes and error resolution for defects or malfunctions arising from existing functionality during the AMC period.
  3. Priority support for incidents reported by the Customer, per the SLAs defined in Section 5.
  4. Remote technical support for system issues, database recovery from valid existing backups, configuration assistance, and troubleshooting.
  5. Minor configuration changes within existing functionality (campaign tweaks, user setup, role permissions).
  6. Annual application version upgrade of the Call Centre application to the next maintenance release, at a mutually agreed maintenance window. Major architectural migrations are excluded and quoted separately.
  7. Application-layer security patching — up to two (2) scheduled patching windows per AMC year. Critical out-of-band security advisories will be assessed case-by-case and applied where the Vendor determines material risk exists.
  8. SSL installation assistance during the AMC term (Customer supplies the certificate; Vendor installs and reloads services).
  9. Firewall hardening recommendations for the DMS/PBX-facing firewall (written recommendations; implementation by Customer IT).
  10. 30 days' prior written notification of any Vendor-supplied license or certificate expiry.
  11. Three (3) on-site visits per AMC year within Kuwait at no additional charge; additional on-site visits will be billed at the Vendor's then-prevailing rate.

3. Exclusions — Not Covered

The following are outside the scope of this AMC and subject to separate written scope and quotation:

  • New feature development, new modules, custom reports, integrations with new third-party systems, or any enhancement beyond the existing functionality of the deployed build.
  • Server hardware maintenance, spare parts, and firmware/BIOS servicing — covered under the manufacturer/distributor warranty (currently Redington for the Lenovo ThinkSystem ST550).
  • Operating-system level administration and patching — remains with Customer IT.
  • Network infrastructure, firewall configuration, internet connectivity, and cyber-attack prevention — sits with Customer IT (server is hosted at Customer premises).
  • SSL certificate procurement and renewal — Customer-owned; the Vendor provides installation assistance and 30-day expiry notification only.
  • Third-party software, services, licenses or APIs not supplied by the Vendor (telco carrier, payment gateways, SMS gateways, etc.).
  • Backup infrastructure hardware, off-site backup, and Disaster Recovery site infrastructure — covered under the separate Synology Backup Solution order between the parties.
  • Issues caused by Customer-side changes, unauthorised modifications, or third-party intervention to the deployed system.
  • Data entry, content migration, or day-to-day operational tasks.
  • Training of new users beyond the initial handover training (refresher / new-batch training quoted separately).
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, telecom outages, power failures, etc.).

4. Service Availability

The Vendor shall make commercially reasonable efforts to ensure the deployed application layer remains available during defined support hours (Section 5). Target application availability is 98% per calendar month, measured during support hours and excluding scheduled maintenance, annual upgrade and patching windows, and downtime caused by Customer-managed infrastructure (server hardware, OS, internet, power, internal network, third-party services, SSL expiry) or Force Majeure events (Section 20).

Resolution targets are commercially reasonable best-effort commitments, not contractual guarantees.

6. Support Hours & Ticket-Based Request Process

6.1 Included support effort

  • 40 hours of remote support per AMC year, covering bug fixes, minor configuration changes, troubleshooting, and Vendor-side work within the AMC scope.
  • Unused hours do not carry forward to the next AMC year.
  • On-site visits (3 per year) are logged separately and do not consume the 40 hours.
  • Effort spent on P1 incident recovery is not counted against the 40-hour pool.
  • Effort required beyond the pool is quoted separately at the Vendor's prevailing consultancy rate before work commences.

6.2 Ticket-based request process (mandatory)

  • All support requests must be raised via a written ticket to the Vendor's designated support channel: support@centrixplus.com.
  • The ticket must include: brief description of the issue, affected module/application, suggested priority level (P1–P4), reproduction steps or screenshots where applicable, and Customer contact for follow-up.
  • SLA response times start from the timestamp of the received ticket — not from verbal requests, WhatsApp messages, phone calls, or direct communication with individual engineers.
  • Verbal / informal requests will be actioned on best-effort basis but do not trigger SLA obligations until a formal ticket is raised.
  • A single named Technical SPOC on the Customer side is nominated as the authorised requestor for all AMC tickets.

7. Vendor Responsibilities

  1. Provide support services in accordance with the scope (Section 2), SLAs (Section 5), and availability commitments (Section 4).
  2. Assign qualified personnel familiar with the deployed solution to handle Customer incidents and requests.
  3. Maintain strict confidentiality of all Customer data and information accessed in the course of providing support, in line with Section 14 and the executed NDA.
  4. Communicate proactively with the Customer during P1/P2 incidents, including periodic status updates until resolution.
  5. Provide the Customer with the agreed periodic reports (Section 13).
  6. Notify the Customer at least 30 days in advance of any Vendor-supplied license expiry or planned major maintenance.
  7. Use Customer-provided credentials and access strictly for the purpose of fulfilling AMC obligations, applying the principle of least privilege.
  8. Not introduce code, components, or configuration changes into the production environment without Customer awareness and a documented change request, save for break-fix activity during active P1 incidents.

8. Customer Responsibilities

  1. Provide timely access (remote and/or on-site) to the systems, networks, and personnel necessary for the Vendor to render support.
  2. Physical security of the production server, including premises access control, locked server room/cabinet, fire/flood protection, power continuity (UPS/backup power), and protection against unauthorised physical access, theft, or vandalism.
  3. Network and cyber-security perimeter defence. Prevention of cyber-attacks, hacking attempts, unauthorised remote intrusions, denial-of-service, and other network-borne threats is the responsibility of the Customer's IT team — including firewall configuration and monitoring, intrusion detection/prevention, endpoint protection, VPN management, and periodic vulnerability scanning.
  4. Server hardware maintenance (via Redington/Lenovo warranty), internet connectivity, internal network, and OS-level administration.
  5. Maintain valid SSL certificates and renew them in time in coordination with the Vendor's reminder notifications.
  6. Maintain regular backups of databases and recordings (via the separately supplied Synology Backup Solution once installed). The Vendor will assist with recovery from existing valid backups but is not responsible for data losses due to absence or corruption of Customer-managed backups.
  7. Promptly report incidents through the agreed ticket channel (Section 6) with sufficient detail to enable diagnosis.
  8. Not allow unauthorised third parties to modify the deployed system without written approval from the Vendor; such modifications may void AMC coverage for affected components.
  9. Nominate and maintain a single Technical SPOC as the authorised AMC requestor.
  10. Pay AMC fees as per Section 15 on or before the due date.

9. Data Protection & Restrictions

  1. All Customer data residing in the deployed solution is and remains the sole property of the Customer.
  2. The Vendor shall access Customer data only to the extent necessary to perform support obligations, and not for any other purpose.
  3. The Vendor shall not copy, extract, transfer, or transmit Customer data outside the Customer's environment without the Customer's prior written consent, except for diagnostic snippets minimally required to troubleshoot a reported issue.
  4. Any Customer data accessed by the Vendor shall be handled in accordance with the executed NDA and applicable data-protection norms in the State of Kuwait.
  5. On termination of this AMC, the Vendor shall not retain copies of Customer data; any diagnostic exports collected during the term will be securely deleted.

10. Credential Management

  1. Access credentials provided by the Customer to the Vendor shall be used by named Vendor personnel only, on the principle of least privilege.
  2. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) shall be enabled on all privileged access accounts issued to the Vendor.
  3. All Vendor sessions with the production environment shall be audit-logged.
  4. The Vendor shall not share Customer credentials with any third party.
  5. The Customer may rotate or revoke any credential at any time; the Vendor will require new credentials to continue providing support thereafter.
  6. The Vendor shall notify the Customer of any change in Vendor personnel having access to Customer systems, so the Customer may rotate credentials accordingly.
  7. Credentials shall be reviewed jointly at least once per AMC year and rotated during the annual maintenance window.

11. License & System Control

  1. The Call Centre Solution is licensed to the Customer for use at the agreed deployment location and user/seat scale. The Customer shall not redistribute, sub-licence, sell, or otherwise transfer the Call Centre Solution to any third party.
  2. License keys and seat counts are controlled by the Vendor. Any change in seat count, additional locations, or new feature modules requires a written addendum and may carry additional licence or AMC charges.
  3. The Vendor shall provide written notification to the Customer at least 30 days before the expiry of any vendor-supplied licence, certificate, or subscription that the Vendor has issued or controls.
  4. The CRM and Mobile Application, developed exclusively for the Customer, are governed by the IP terms in Section 14.
  5. The Customer shall not modify, reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Vendor-supplied code, save to the extent permitted by mandatory law.
  6. The Customer shall not provide or expose Vendor source code, administrator credentials, or system-level access to any third party (including competing vendors) without the Vendor's prior written consent, save to the extent required by mandatory law.

12. Source-Code Escrow / Emergency Access

An emergency-access framework may be activated in the event of Vendor insolvency, cessation of support, or material breach of this AMC. Activation triggers, escrow custodian, and release conditions shall be documented in a separate mutually-signed Escrow Schedule. Absent such a schedule, no escrow arrangement is in force.

13. Reporting

  1. The Vendor shall provide a quarterly support report summarising tickets raised, incidents resolved, SLA performance against the targets in Section 5, and any pending items.
  2. An annual AMC review will be held prior to renewal, covering operational performance, open improvement items, and planned upgrades.
  3. Ad-hoc reports outside this standard cadence may be requested and will be provided on best-effort basis within reasonable timelines.

14. Intellectual Property, Confidentiality & Security

  1. The Call Centre Solution remains the proprietary product of the Vendor. Nothing in this AMC transfers ownership.
  2. The CRM & Mobile Application has been developed exclusively for the Customer; the Vendor warrants it will not be sold, licensed, sub-licensed or distributed to any third party.
  3. Both parties shall observe the confidentiality obligations set out in the executed Non-Disclosure Agreement, which forms an integral part of this engagement.
  4. The Vendor shall apply reasonable technical and organisational security measures to any Customer data or credentials in its possession, including encrypted storage of access keys, restricted personnel access, and prompt revocation upon staff changes.
  5. Either party shall promptly notify the other in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected security breach involving the deployed solution or Customer data, and cooperate in investigation and remediation.
  6. Confidentiality obligations under this AMC and the NDA shall survive termination of this AMC for a period of three (3) years; perpetual survival is retained only for identified trade-secret material.

15. AMC Fees & Payment Terms

The Customer shall select one of the two options in Section 17 (Selected Package) below. The annual AMC fee corresponding to the selected option shall apply.

  1. Annual AMC fee is payable in advance at the start of each AMC year.
  2. Invoice will be raised by the Vendor at the commencement of the AMC year; payment is due within 15 days of invoice date.
  3. Late payment beyond 30 days from the due date may, at the Vendor's discretion, result in suspension of priority support until cleared.
  4. All fees are exclusive of any applicable taxes, which shall be borne by the Customer.

16. Term, Renewal & Termination

  1. This AMC is valid for one (1) year from the Effective Date and shall be reviewed for renewal at least 30 days before expiry.
  2. Either party may terminate this AMC by giving the other party 30 days' prior written notice. Pre-paid AMC fees for the unexpired portion shall be refunded on a pro-rata basis less any work already rendered.
  3. The Vendor will not suspend support during good-faith commercial disputes. After 60 days of unresolved breach plus 14 days' written notice, the Vendor may suspend non-critical (P3/P4) support only. P1/P2 critical support continues throughout.
  4. On termination, the Vendor shall hand over current production configurations and any Customer-owned data; software licences and proprietary code remain governed by Section 14.

19. Limitation of Liability

The Vendor's total aggregate liability under this AMC, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the greater of (a) twelve (12) months' AMC fees paid by the Customer, or (b) fifty percent (50%) of the total Software Development cost under the SDA dated 02 April 2025. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages.

The above liability cap does not apply to: fraud, willful misconduct, intellectual property infringement, intentional security breach, or breach of confidentiality obligations — for which liability remains uncapped.

20. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform obligations under this AMC due to events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, war, civil unrest, telecommunications failures, governmental restrictions, or pandemic measures.

21. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This AMC shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Kuwait. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this AMC shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably; failing which, the courts of Kuwait shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

22. Change Requests

Any work requested by the Customer that falls outside the scope of this AMC (as defined in Sections 2 and 3) shall be initiated through a written Change Request and shall be subject to a separate scope, timeline, and quotation, mutually agreed before any work commences.

23. Entire Agreement

This AMC, together with the Software Development Agreement dated 02 April 2025 and the executed Non-Disclosure Agreement, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the maintenance and support of the deployed solution. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
