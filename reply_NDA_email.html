Summary of Key Proposed Amendments

High-priority items:

  • Section 7 (Term): Reduce confidentiality survival period from five (5) years to three (3) years, with perpetual protection retained for genuine trade secrets. This aligns with standard international vendor NDA practice.
  • Section 10 (Remedies): Add a defined liability cap (limited to fees paid under the AMC in the preceding 12 months), exclude indirect/consequential damages, and clarify that any liquidated damages must be agreed in writing per incident. Carve-outs are retained for fraud and gross negligence so Frontline keeps full recourse in serious scenarios.
  • Section 6 (Data Ownership): Add an explicit Vendor IP carve-out confirming that Centrix Plus retains ownership of its proprietary software, source code, configurations, and tools. Frontline's ownership of customer, banking, shipment, delivery, call recording, and operational data is fully preserved.
  • Section 5 (No Disruption to Operations): Add carve-outs for agreed scheduled maintenance windows, third-party/hardware failures, and Force Majeure — aligning with the 98% uptime target already discussed under the AMC.
  • Section 12 (Entire Agreement): Clarify that this NDA supplements (and does not supersede) the AMC Agreement, with the AMC governing commercial terms in case of conflict.

Additional refinements (medium / low priority):

  • Section 2: Add standard exclusions from "Confidential Information" (publicly available info, prior knowledge, independent development, legally compelled disclosure).
  • Section 4: Add an emergency-response exception to the Production Change Control requirement, so that during active security incidents or outages we can act to restore service immediately and report within 24 hours.
  • Section 8: Carve-out for records the Vendor is legally required to retain (Kuwait / UAE FZCO regulatory and tax obligations).
  • Section 11: Add an arbitration clause (KCAC, Kuwait City, English language) for cross-border enforceability.
  • New Section 13: Permitted Disclosures clause — allowing internal sharing with Centrix Plus engineers/representatives on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations.
  • New Section 14: Defined notice/communications method (email to designated representatives) for any approvals required under the Agreement.
Important: All clauses relating to Call Recording Protection, Data Handling & Backup Restrictions, Live Data Usage Restriction, VPN & Remote Access Security, API & Integration Security, Access Revocation Upon Employee Exit, Remote Support Monitoring, Public Disclosure Restriction, and No License or Ownership are accepted by Centrix Plus in their entirety, without modification.
