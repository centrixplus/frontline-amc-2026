Executive Summary

Frontline's counter-version of the AMC has been carefully reviewed. While the core structure of our v3 has been preserved, the customer has loaded the contract with significant additional obligations, tightened SLAs to enterprise-grade levels, extended liability beyond commercial proportionality, and removed the Vendor's ability to enforce payment terms. For the unchanged annual fee of KD 1,000, the total cost of delivery now substantially exceeds the AMC value. Centrix Plus is prepared to accept many proposed clarifications, but the items marked High must be revised to make the agreement commercially executable.
