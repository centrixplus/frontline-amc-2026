AMC Counter-Response

Re: Annual Maintenance Contract — Centrix Plus Integrated Solution & Frontline Express Company
Reference: Frontline-updated AMC draft received 23 May 2026
Prepared by: Centrix Plus Integrated Solution — FZCO
Date: 23 May 2026
Status: Counter-Proposal — Pending Frontline Review

Centrix Plus has carefully reviewed Frontline's revised AMC draft. We appreciate the depth of the customer's review and the operational rigour it reflects. Most of the additions strengthen the document and we are pleased to accept them. However, eight (8) clauses materially change the commercial balance of the agreement and require revision before signature. This counter-response sets out each amendment with proposed final language and rationale.

Centrix Plus accepts the following Frontline additions without further negotiation:
  • Section 10.6 — MFA mandatory for privileged + remote access
  • Section 10.7, 10.10, 10.11, 10.13, 10.14 — Strengthened credential management controls
  • Section 12 — Source Code Escrow / Emergency Access (as drafted — "subject to mutual agreement")
  • Section 8.5 — Vendor provides firewall hardening recommendations for DMS/PBX
  • Section 8.8 — Reasonable SSL installation assistance during AMC term
  • Section 8.3 — Patching frequency increased from once to twice per AMC year
  • Section 19 — Liability carve-outs (fraud, willful misconduct, IP infringement, intentional security breach, confidentiality breach)

Proposed Amendments

Amendment 1 — Section 5 (SLA Response & Resolution Times) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

SeverityResponseResolution
P1 — System Down30 minutes2 hours
P2 — Major Function Impact1 hour6 hours
P3 — Partial Service4 hours2 business days
P4 — Minor / Enhancement1 business day4 business days
Plus: P1 supported on 24×7 basis including weekends and public holidays.

Centrix Counter:

SeverityResponseResolution (best effort)
P1 — System Down1 working hour (24×7 acknowledgement)1 working day

P3 — Partial Service1 working day5 working days
P4 — Minor / Enhancement2 working daysBest effort

Rationale: A 2-hour P1 resolution commitment requires dedicated 24×7 engineering rotation — a cost structure that significantly exceeds the KD 1,000 annual AMC fee. The proposed counter retains 24×7 acknowledgement (so Frontline always knows an outage is being worked on), aligns with telecom-industry small-vendor norms, and remains commercially executable. If Frontline genuinely requires sub-2-hour P1 resolution, Centrix Plus would be pleased to quote a premium 24×7 support package separately.

Amendment 2 — Section 8.2 (Hardware & OS Warranty) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

"All hardware delivered by The Vendor, including the DMS and PBX Server, and Backup Server, shall be covered under warranty, service, and support for a period of three (3) years. This coverage shall include, but not be limited to, server hardware, operating systems (OS), security patches, system security updates, and related support services."

Centrix Counter:

"Hardware delivered by the Vendor (DMS, PBX, and Backup servers) shall be covered by the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) warranty for the period offered by the OEM at the time of supply (typically 12 months). Extended hardware support beyond the OEM warranty period is available as a separately priced annual addendum.

Operating-system patching and OS-level maintenance remain the responsibility of the Customer's IT team, consistent with Section 8.1. The Vendor will apply application-layer security patches twice per AMC year as part of the scheduled maintenance window (Section 2.8)."

Rationale: Hardware warranties are determined by the OEM (Dell, HP, Lenovo, etc.) — Centrix Plus cannot extend the manufacturer's warranty beyond what the OEM offers. A 3-year vendor-backed warranty including OS patching effectively transforms the AMC into a hardware-services contract, which is a fundamentally different commercial product. This amendment also resolves the internal conflict between Sections 8.1 and 8.2 (OS responsibility).

Amendment 3 — Section 9.9 (Confidentiality Survival Period) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

"The confidentiality, data protection, and operational security obligations stated herein shall survive the termination or expiry of the AMC agreement and shall remain binding on the Vendor thereafter."

Centrix Counter:

"The confidentiality, data protection, and operational security obligations stated herein shall survive the termination or expiry of the AMC agreement for a period of three (3) years; provided that obligations relating to information constituting a 'trade secret' under applicable Kuwait law shall survive for so long as such information retains its trade-secret status. This clause harmonises with Section 14.6."

Rationale: A perpetual confidentiality obligation is rare in international vendor contracts and creates indefinite legal exposure. The 3-year period aligns with standard practice and with Section 14.6 of the same document, eliminating the internal conflict. Trade-secret information receives perpetual protection (the carve-out preserves Frontline's strongest interests).

Amendment 4 — Section 10.15 (Kuwait-Only Access Restriction) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

"Access to the DMS and PBX system software shall be restricted to users accessing from Kuwait only. Any request for access from a new location, country, branch office, VPN endpoint, or external network shall require prior written approval from Customer IT team before access is granted."

Centrix Counter:

"Access to the DMS and PBX system software shall be restricted to named authorised Vendor personnel, regardless of geographic location, connecting through the Vendor's approved secure VPN with multi-factor authentication. Centrix Plus will provide Customer's IT team with a list of authorised personnel and notify of any additions/removals within 5 business days. All remote sessions remain logged and subject to monitoring as per Section 4 of the NDA."

Rationale: Centrix Plus is a UAE FZCO entity and supports clients across the GCC. A pure geo-restriction makes day-to-day remote support operationally infeasible (a Centrix engineer travelling for a separate client cannot respond to a P1 incident). The proposed counter achieves the same security objective — restricting access to known, named, authenticated personnel — without imposing an unworkable location constraint.

Amendment 5 — Section 15.4 & 17.4 (Payment & Service-Suspension Leverage) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

15.4: "The Vendor shall not disable, suspend, restrict, encrypt, lock, block, or otherwise interfere with operational access... during AMC disputes, payment discussions, renewal discussions, or any commercial disagreement."

17.4: "Late payment beyond 30 days from the due date may, at the Vendor's discretion, result only in suspension of priority/non-critical support services until payment is cleared. However, such delay shall not affect or interrupt the Customer's operational activities, system access, software usage, database access, customer data, delivery operations, call center operations, or normal business operations."

Centrix Counter:

15.4 (revised): "The Vendor shall not disable, suspend, restrict, encrypt, lock, block, or otherwise interfere with operational access to the Customer's systems during good-faith disputes under active negotiation. Where Customer has materially breached payment obligations or other Agreement terms and remains in unresolved breach for more than sixty (60) days without active dialogue, the Vendor retains the right to suspend non-critical support services after fourteen (14) days' prior written notice. Critical (P1) support shall continue. This clause does not limit either party's termination rights under Section 18."

17.4 (revised): "Late payment beyond sixty (60) days from the invoice due date may, at the Vendor's discretion and after 14 days' written notice, result in suspension of non-critical (P3/P4) support services until payment is cleared. Critical (P1) and high-priority (P2) support shall continue. Continued non-payment beyond ninety (90) days entitles the Vendor to terminate this Agreement under Section 18 and pursue legal remedies."

Rationale: As Frontline's version is drafted, the Vendor has zero contractual remedy for non-payment — Frontline could withhold the KD 1,000 fee indefinitely while continuing to receive service. The revised version protects Frontline's operational continuity during good-faith disputes (no abrupt cutoffs, no encryption/locking of systems) while preserving the Vendor's basic commercial leverage in the case of clear, sustained breach. P1/P2 critical support continues throughout to protect Frontline's customers.

Amendment 6 — Section 16 (Backup & Disaster Recovery Support) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

"The Vendor shall be responsible for the restoration and recovery of the software applications and operating system in the event of a disaster, system failure or a similar incident. The vendor shall provide the necessary hardware infrastructure and valid backup data required for the recovery process..."

Centrix Counter:

"Within the scope of this AMC, the Vendor shall provide reasonable assistance to recover the deployed software applications from valid, current backups supplied by the Customer, following any disaster, system failure, or comparable incident. The Vendor will undertake reinstallation, reconfiguration, and restoration activities on the Customer-supplied hardware to bring the application layer back to operational state within a reasonable timeframe.

Operating system reinstallation and configuration remain within Customer IT scope.

Comprehensive backup & disaster-recovery infrastructure (backup hardware, off-site replication, RTO/RPO commitments, scheduled backup verification, retention rotation) is covered under the separate Synology Backup Solution quoted by the Vendor to the Customer and is not included in this AMC."

Rationale: Recovery from valid existing backups is appropriate AMC scope (Centrix Plus already includes this informally). However, the broader B&DR infrastructure responsibilities — hardware, off-site replication, RTO/RPO guarantees — are properly priced and delivered through the separate Synology solution already under discussion. Including both in the same KD 1,000 AMC creates double-counting and erodes margin from both products. This amendment preserves both vendor and customer's intended commercial structure.

Amendment 7 — Section 18.4 (3-Year Price Freeze) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

"The Vendor guarantees that the pricing for the services provided under this Agreement shall remain fixed and unchanged for a period of three (3) years from the effective date."

Centrix Counter (Option A — Recommended):

"The AMC fee shall remain fixed at KD 1,000 for the first AMC year. For the second and third AMC years, the fee may be revised upon mutual agreement, subject to a maximum annual increase of the Kuwait Consumer Price Index (CPI) increase or 5%, whichever is lower. Any revision shall be notified by the Vendor at least 60 days prior to renewal."

Centrix Counter (Option B — Alternative):

"If a 3-year price freeze is required, the annual AMC fee shall be revised to KD 1,500 per year, fixed for three (3) years from the effective date, to absorb future cost increases."

Rationale: Locking the price for 3 years while the scope of obligations has materially expanded (24×7 P1, OS patching, 4 on-site visits, MFA infrastructure, etc.) creates an annual real-terms loss for the Vendor. The proposed counter offers two commercially viable paths — either modest CPI-capped annual review, or a higher locked fee that covers the expanded obligations.

Amendment 8 — Section 19 (Liability Cap) HIGH

Frontline's Proposal:

"The Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this AMC or initial product development agreement... shall not exceed an amount equal to the total direct software Development costs + AMC fee paid or payable by Customer to the Vendor."

Centrix Counter:

"The Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this AMC, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, or otherwise, shall not exceed the greater of (a) the AMC fees paid by the Customer in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim, or (b) fifty percent (50%) of the total direct software Development costs paid by Customer to Vendor.

The liability carve-outs proposed by Customer (breach of confidentiality, willful misconduct, gross negligence, fraud, intentional security breaches, IP infringement) are accepted in full and shall remain uncapped.

In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, punitive, or loss-of-profit damages, except in cases involving fraud, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality obligations."

Rationale: Frontline's proposed cap (Development cost + AMC fee, with no time decay) creates indefinite liability exposure tied to a one-time development cost. Standard international IT-vendor practice is 12 months' fees or a defined fraction of development cost. The counter retains Frontline's important uncapped carve-outs for genuine bad-faith scenarios (fraud, confidentiality breach, IP infringement) while ensuring routine commercial disputes remain proportionate to the contract value.

Minor / Drafting Amendments

Amendment 9 — Section 2.8 (OS Patching Reference) MEDIUM

Delete "and OS" from Section 2.8. OS patching remains Customer scope (resolves conflict with Section 8.1).

Amendment 10 — Section 2.9 (On-site Visits) MEDIUM

Adjust from 4 visits/year to 3 visits/year as commercial middle ground.

Amendment 11 — Section 3 (Exclusions) MEDIUM

Restore the following exclusions removed by Customer: (a) Server hardware and operating system, (b) SSL certificate procurement and renewal (installation assistance is accepted under Section 8.8), (c) Backup & Disaster Recovery infrastructure (Synology solution covers this). Define or remove "standard client integrations" phrase to prevent unbounded scope.

Amendment 12 — Section 5 (Escalation Matrix) LOW

Centrix Plus will provide a single named Technical SPOC (Technical Manager) and a group support email, rather than four named individuals across four escalation levels. A small vendor cannot operationally sustain a 4-tier named escalation matrix.

Amendment 13 — Section 13 & 15 (Numbering / Duplicates) LOW

Re-number Section 13 to start at item 1. Remove duplicate items 1–3 from Section 15 (currently copy-pasted from Section 13 Reporting). No commercial impact — purely document cleanup.

Closing Statement

Centrix Plus is committed to building a long-term, professional, and transparent partnership with Frontline Express. The amendments proposed above are not a rejection of Frontline's revisions — most of the customer's additions are accepted in full. The eight high-priority items, however, fundamentally change the commercial balance of the agreement and must be revised to ensure the AMC remains executable in good faith and proportionate to the contracted fee.

We are happy to discuss any of these items in a brief call, and we are open to creative middle-ground solutions on each (including a re-priced premium-support package if Frontline genuinely requires enterprise-grade 24×7 SLAs).

We look forward to Frontline's feedback at the earliest convenience so that we may finalise and sign both the AMC and the NDA together.
